The short answer

You do not need to be a big company to offer strong health benefits. Small businesses today have three main paths: a traditional group plan, an ICHRA that reimburses employees tax-free for their own plans, or a simpler QSEHRA for teams under 50. The right choice depends on your budget, your team, and how hands-on you want to be. We help you compare all three and get a quote for free.

Your Options

3 Ways to Offer Health Insurance to Your Team

We compare every option so you only pay for what makes sense for your business.

G

Group Health Plan

One health plan you offer to all eligible employees, with the business and employees sharing the premium. Great for teams that want traditional, familiar coverage with strong carrier networks.

  • Predictable, well-known coverage
  • You control the plan and contribution
  • Often available from just a few employees
Most familiar
I

ICHRA

An Individual Coverage HRA lets you give employees a tax-free monthly allowance to buy their own individual plans. You control the budget, they choose the plan that fits them best.

  • You set a fixed, predictable budget
  • Employees pick their own plan
  • No group size minimum
Most flexible
Q

QSEHRA

A Qualified Small Employer HRA is a simple reimbursement option for businesses with fewer than 50 employees. You reimburse employees tax-free for premiums and qualified expenses.

  • Simple to set up and run
  • Built for small teams under 50
  • Tax-free reimbursements
Simplest for small teams

Common Questions

Small Business Health Insurance FAQ

How much does small business health insurance cost?

It depends on your team size, employee ages, location, and the plan level you choose. Many small businesses split the premium with employees to keep it affordable, and contributions are often tax deductible. We compare carriers to find the lowest cost for the coverage you want, at no charge.

Do small businesses have to offer health insurance?

Businesses with fewer than 50 full-time employees are not required to offer health insurance. Many still do because it is one of the strongest tools for recruiting and keeping good employees, and the cost can be tax deductible.

What is the difference between a group plan, ICHRA, and QSEHRA?

A group plan is one health plan the business offers to all eligible employees. An ICHRA lets you reimburse employees tax-free for individual plans they choose. A QSEHRA is a simpler reimbursement option for businesses with fewer than 50 employees. We help you pick the model that fits your budget and team.

How many employees do I need to get a plan?

In most states you can start a group health plan with as few as one or two employees, and reimbursement options like ICHRA and QSEHRA can work for very small teams too. We find the right fit no matter your size.

Is small business health insurance tax deductible?

In most cases the premiums a business pays toward employee coverage are tax deductible as a business expense, and some small businesses qualify for the Small Business Health Care Tax Credit. Ask your tax advisor about your specific situation.
