The problem: hauling now, getting paid later

Brokers and shippers commonly pay freight invoices on 30- to 60-day terms. Fuel, tolls, truck payments and insurance do not wait that long. Factoring closes the gap: you sell the invoice to a factoring company and get the cash up front.

The catch with a lot of factoring is the waiting that is still left. Paperwork goes in, the load gets verified, the payment gets processed, and if you dropped on a Friday night or a holiday, it sits until someone is back at a desk.

What DropPay does differently

  • 24/7/365 funding. No cutoff times. Nights, weekends and holidays included.
  • Paid within minutes after drop-off. You no longer have to wait for verifications and processing.
  • Free fuel advances so you can get to the next load.
  • No hidden fees and simple online account management.

Frequently asked

What is DropPay?

DropPay is a freight factoring program from Express Freight Finance. You sell your freight invoice and get paid right after you drop off the load, instead of waiting 30 to 60 days for the broker or shipper to pay.

How fast does DropPay pay?

Express Freight Finance funds DropPay 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year, with no cutoff times. Drivers get paid within minutes after dropping off a load, without waiting on the usual verification and processing.
