How to Set Up Group Health Insurance for Your Florida Small Business

A step-by-step 2026 guide to setting up group health insurance for your Florida small business: who qualifies, plan types, carriers, timeline, and mistakes to avoid. Free quote.

Does your Florida business qualify?

Setting up group health insurance in Florida is more accessible than most owners think. In Florida a "small group" is generally 1–50 employees, and most carriers require at least 2 enrolled employees — usually including one non-owner W-2 employee. You do not need an HR department, and you can start in any month because group coverage has no open enrollment window.

Step-by-step: offering coverage to your team

  1. Gather a census. Collect each employee's date of birth, ZIP code, and dependent count. This is what carriers quote from.
  2. Decide your budget. Choose how much of the premium you'll cover (at least 50% of employee-only is typical).
  3. Pick a plan type. Fully insured, level-funded, or ICHRA — see below.
  4. Compare carriers. Networks vary by Florida county, so this step matters most.
  5. Enroll your team. Employees select coverage; your advisor handles the paperwork and setup.
  6. Manage renewals. Re-shop every year rather than auto-renewing.

Choosing a plan structure

Fully insured

The traditional fixed-premium model with a carrier like Florida Blue or Cigna. Simple and predictable — best for smaller or first-time groups.

Level-funded

Fast-growing in the 2–50 space. You pay a fixed monthly amount, get claims data, and can receive money back in healthy years. Best for healthier teams that want transparency.

ICHRA

Give employees a tax-free monthly allowance to buy their own ACA plan. Great flexibility and no minimum participation. Best for mixed or variable-hour workforces.

Florida carriers to compare

Because networks differ by county, matching the carrier to where your employees live is the single biggest quality-of-coverage decision:

  • Florida Blue — all 67 counties (BlueOptions PPO, myBlue HMO)
  • Cigna & UnitedHealthcare — strong in Miami-Dade, Broward, Hillsborough, Orange
  • Humana, Aetna, AvMed — competitive South and Central Florida options

How long does it take?

A Florida group plan can typically be set up in a few weeks. Because most carriers have a 30-day implementation window, start about 30–45 days before your target start date. There's no need to wait for any enrollment season.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Auto-renewing without benchmarking other carriers.
  • Ignoring the tax credit — see if you qualify for the Small Business Health Care Tax Credit.
  • Choosing on premium alone instead of total cost including deductibles.
  • Picking a national broker who doesn't know Florida county networks.

Frequently asked questions

How do I set up health insurance for my small business in Florida?

Gather an employee census, decide your contribution budget, choose a plan type, compare Florida carriers, and enroll your team. A licensed advisor can handle the setup for you at no cost — you can start in any month.

How many employees do I need?

Generally at least 2 enrolled employees, usually including one non-owner W-2 employee. Florida small-group plans cover 1–50 employees.

When can I start a plan?

Any time. Group health insurance has no open enrollment window, so you can set up coverage year-round.
