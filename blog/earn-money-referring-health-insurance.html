How to Earn Money Referring People for Health Insurance

You probably know several people who are overpaying for coverage, uninsured, or unhappy with their plan. Pointing them to the right help can put real money in your pocket. Here is exactly how getting paid to refer health insurance works, how much you can earn, and how to start for free.

The short version
Refer someone who needs health insurance to VS Health Benefits. When they enroll, you earn a referral payout. It is free to join, you need no license, and there is no limit on how much you can make. The only requirement is being a VS Health Benefits client yourself. See the Refer and Earn program.

How Getting Paid to Refer Health Insurance Works

The idea is simple. Health insurance is something almost everyone needs, but most people do not know where to find honest help. When you connect someone to a licensed advisor who finds them the right plan, that introduction has real value, and our Refer and Earn program pays you for it.

You do not sell anything, quote anything, or fill out any insurance paperwork. You simply submit the person's name and contact details through your member portal. Our licensed advisors reach out, compare plans, and handle the entire enrollment. When their coverage goes active, your referral payout is on the way. The more people you refer, the more you earn, with no cap.

How Much Money Can You Make

There is no ceiling on your earnings. You receive a payout for every referral who enrolls in coverage, so your income grows with each person you help. Refer one friend and you earn once. Refer ten people over a few months and you earn ten times. Many members treat it as steady extra income on the side, with zero overhead and nothing to buy.

What drives your earnings

  • Number of referrals. Every enrolled referral pays. The single biggest lever is simply sending more.
  • Quality of the introduction. People who genuinely need coverage are more likely to enroll, which means more payouts for you.
  • Consistency. Members who send a referral whenever the topic of insurance comes up earn the most over time.

You can watch every referral move from In Progress to Sold to Paid right inside your member portal, so you always know exactly what you have earned.

"The people who earn the most are not salespeople. They are everyday clients who simply mention us when a friend complains about their coverage. One honest introduction at the right moment is all it takes."

Who Can Earn, and the One Requirement

Anyone can refer. You do not need experience, a license, or a background in insurance. The only requirement to receive payouts is that you are a VS Health Benefits client yourself.

That requirement is easy to meet and it costs you nothing extra. Becoming a client just means letting our advisors help you find and enroll in your own coverage through the brokerage. It is free guidance, it usually saves people money, and once you are covered you unlock the ability to earn on every referral. If you are not a client yet, request a free quote and we will help you get set up first.

How to Start in Four Steps

From sign up to paid

  • 1. Create your free account. Sign up for a referral account in under two minutes at the member portal. No fees, no commitment.
  • 2. Set up your profile. Add your details and how you would like to be paid. This is your earnings dashboard.
  • 3. Submit a referral. Share the name and contact info of anyone who needs coverage. We take it from there.

Who to Refer

The best referrals are people who already need help. You almost certainly know several of them. Think about who in your life fits these groups:

  • 1099 workers and self-employed people who do not get coverage through an employer.
  • Truck drivers and gig workers who are on the road and need a plan that travels with them.
  • Families who are paying too much or worried about deductibles.
  • Small business owners who want to offer their team coverage.
  • Anyone uninsured, newly unemployed, or unhappy with their current plan.

You are not pitching anyone. You are doing them a favor by connecting them to free, honest help, and you get paid when it works out.

Why Referrals Work So Well for Insurance

Health insurance is confusing, and people trust recommendations from someone they know far more than an ad. When a friend says "these people actually helped me and saved me money," that warm introduction converts much better than cold marketing. That is why the payout exists. Your trusted word is genuinely valuable, and the program shares that value with you.

Tips to Earn More

  • Mention it naturally. Whenever insurance, doctor bills, or money comes up, let people know you can connect them to free help.
  • Share your portal link. Make it easy for people to reach out by pointing them to the Refer and Earn page.
  • Follow up. A quick "did you ever get that coverage sorted?" turns more introductions into enrolled referrals.
  • Refer in groups. Families, coworkers, and small teams often need coverage at the same time.

Is It Legit, and Do You Need a License?

Yes, it is legitimate, and no, you do not need a license. You are simply referring someone to a licensed brokerage. The advisors at VS Health Benefits, who are licensed in 40+ states, do all of the regulated insurance work. Referral payouts are released after a referred applicant is approved for coverage, and everything is tracked transparently in your member portal.
