Employer Questions

Small Business Health Insurance, Answered

How much does small business health insurance cost in 2026?

In Florida, small group coverage commonly runs about $470 to $740 per employee per month for single coverage, depending on the plan tier and your team's average age. Most employers contribute 50% to 75% of that. The calculator above gives you a personalized estimate.

How much do I have to contribute as the employer?

Most carriers require you to pay at least 50% of the employee-only premium and to enroll a minimum share of eligible employees. Many small businesses choose 50% to 70%. We help you pick a contribution that fits your budget and still meets carrier rules.

Is it tax deductible?

Yes. Employer contributions toward employee premiums are generally a deductible business expense, and some small businesses qualify for the Small Business Health Care Tax Credit. Ask your tax advisor about your situation.

How many employees do I need?

In Florida you can set up small group coverage with as few as 2 enrolled employees. We set up groups of every size at no cost to the employer and handle all the paperwork.
