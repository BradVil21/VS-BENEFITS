## Reply scripts

**"How much does it cost?"**
There's no fee to you for my side - carriers pay the commission. The cost is whatever you
decide to put in per person per month, and you set that. What's your headcount and do you
have office staff and field crew, or all field?

**"We use 1099 guys."**
Then this doesn't fit, and I'll say so plainly. It's W-2 only. If any part of the crew is
W-2 - dispatch, office, a few lead techs - it can cover just them. Worth a look?

**"We already have a group plan."**
Then the question is what your renewal came in at. If it's up more than single digits,
worth running the comparison. If it came in flat, keep it - I'll tell you that too.

**"Send me something."**
Sending a PDF now is how this dies in a folder. Give me fifteen minutes on the phone and
I'll send you numbers built on your actual headcount instead of a brochure. Tomorrow at
7am or is after 5 better?

**"Not right now."**
Fair. January 1 or spring? Offering it opens a sixty-day enrollment window for your guys
whenever you do it, so spring is a real option, not a brush-off on my end.
