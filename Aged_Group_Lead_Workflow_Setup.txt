AGED GROUP LEAD WORKFLOW - GHL SETUP (about 10 minutes)

--------------------------------------------------
STEP 1 - CREATE THE FOLDER
Automation > Workflows > Create folder
Name: Aged Group Leads

--------------------------------------------------
STEP 2 - CREATE THE WORKFLOW
Open the Aged Group Leads folder > Create workflow > Start from scratch
Rename it (top left): Aged Group Lead Sequence - 30 Days

--------------------------------------------------
STEP 3 - TRIGGER
Add New Trigger > Contact Tag
Filter: Tag Added = aged group lead
(Type "aged group lead" to create the tag if it doesn't exist yet.)

--------------------------------------------------
STEP 4 - ACTIONS (in this order)

1. Send Email  - Day 1   "the quote you never got"
2. Wait        - 2 days
3. Send Email  - Day 3   "two things"
4. Wait        - 9 days
5. Send Email  - Day 12  "closing your file"
6. Wait        - 18 days
7. Send Email  - Day 30  "how'd the renewal come back?"
8. Add Contact Tag - aged group lead - completed

For each Send Email action:
- From name: Bradley Vilsaint
- From email: your sending address
- Subject and body: copy from Aged_Group_Lead_Emails.txt (keep the subject lowercase)
- Use plain text, no images, links or attachments (the playbook says plain emails get more replies)

For each Wait action, you can also set "Advanced window" to weekdays, 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM so emails don't land on weekends or at night.

--------------------------------------------------
STEP 5 - SETTINGS (gear icon / Settings tab)
- Stop on response: ON   (anyone who replies leaves the sequence so you can work them personally)
- Allow re-entry: OFF
- Time window: Mon-Fri, 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM (contact's timezone)

--------------------------------------------------
STEP 6 - TEST, THEN PUBLISH
- Use "Test workflow" with your own contact (make sure your contact has a company name filled in).
- Check the email looks right, then switch Draft to Publish and Save.

--------------------------------------------------
STEP 7 - START IT
Contacts > filter your old group leads > select them > Add Tag > aged group lead
Tip: start with a batch of 25-50 so replies stay manageable.

--------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU LAUNCH
- Company name must be filled in on each contact, or the email will read "numbers for ," with a blank. Filter out or fix contacts with no company.
- These are old leads, so keep the unsubscribe footer GHL adds on.
- When someone replies with their renewal month, log it and set a task 75 days before that date. That's the whole point of the playbook.

--------------------------------------------------
SHORTCUT - "BUILD USING AI" IN GHL
Automation > Workflows > Build using AI, and paste:

Create a workflow named "Aged Group Lead Sequence - 30 Days". Trigger: Contact Tag added "aged group lead". Actions: Send Email (subject "the quote you never got"), Wait 2 days, Send Email (subject "two things"), Wait 9 days, Send Email (subject "closing your file"), Wait 18 days, Send Email (subject "how'd the renewal come back?"), then Add Tag "aged group lead - completed". Stop the workflow if the contact replies.

Then paste each email body from Aged_Group_Lead_Emails.txt into the matching Send Email step and move the workflow into the Aged Group Leads folder.
