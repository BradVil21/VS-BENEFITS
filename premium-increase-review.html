Seven ways people cut a 2027 increase

These are the levers we check against your renewal. Most people only need one or two of them.

1. Update your income

Your credit is based on the income you expect in 2027. An old or overstated figure can cost you hundreds a month. What income counts.

2. Re-shop against the new benchmark

The second-cheapest silver plan sets your credit. When it changes insurers, the cheapest plan for you often changes too.

3. Pair a bronze plan with an HSA

Every bronze plan is HSA-eligible now. Contributions lower your taxes and the income your credit is based on.

4. Check if your plan was replaced

Cigna is leaving Florida's Marketplace and Sunshine State Health Plan products end in 2026. Replacement plans are rarely the best price.

5. Over the subsidy cliff? Go off-exchange

Above 400% of poverty ($63,840 single) there is no credit, so private and off-exchange PPOs compete on equal terms. Private options.

6. Split the household

Kids may qualify for Florida KidCare, and spouses sometimes do better on different plans.

7. Don't trade your doctors for $50

We check networks and drug lists first, so a lower premium doesn't turn into a surprise bill.

Premium increase questions

Why did my health insurance go up for 2027?

Insurers raised prices again, about 10.4% on average in Florida and a 15% median request nationally, driven by hospital and drug costs and a sicker risk pool after the enhanced tax credits expired. Your own increase also depends on how much your tax credit changed, your age, your income estimate and whether your plan was discontinued.

Is the premium on my renewal letter final?

No. During Open Enrollment, November 1, 2026 to January 15, 2027, you can switch to any plan in your area and update your income. Choose by December 15 for a January 1 start. If you do nothing, you are auto-renewed at the new price.

What do you need from me for the review?

Your ZIP code, who needs coverage, what you paid each month in 2026 and what the 2027 renewal says. A photo of the renewal letter helps but is not required. An advisor will also ask about your expected 2027 income, your doctors and your prescriptions.

My insurer is leaving. Can you help?

Yes. For 2027 in Florida, Cigna is leaving the Marketplace and Sunshine State Health Plan's Marketplace products end, with members who do not choose a plan moved to Ambetter. We compare replacement plans and check your doctors before you are placed in one automatically.

Does the review cost anything?

No. Insurance carriers pay licensed brokers, and Marketplace prices are the same whether you enroll yourself or with us. If your current plan is still your best option, we will tell you.
