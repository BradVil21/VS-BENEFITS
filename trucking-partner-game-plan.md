# Game Plan: Become the Referred Health Insurance Partner for Logistics and Owner-Operator Companies

**Goal:** Position VS Health Benefits as the go-to health insurance partner that logistics companies, fleets, and owner-operator businesses send their drivers to for coverage.

**Prepared for:** Bradley Vilsaint, Founder and CEO, VS Health Benefits

---

## 1. Why This Works

Truckers are one of the most underserved groups in health insurance:

- Most owner-operators and lease drivers are 1099, so no employer covers their health insurance.
- They are on the road constantly, so shopping for coverage is a hassle and most put it off.
- They earn well, which means they can afford good coverage but rarely have someone guiding them.
- One accident or illness without coverage can end a driver's career and cost a fleet a truck.

Logistics companies feel this pain too. Driver turnover is brutal and expensive. A company that helps its drivers get health coverage looks like it cares, which helps recruit and keep drivers. That is your opening: you solve a problem for the driver and a retention problem for the company at the same time, at no cost to the company.

You already have the pieces in place:

- A dedicated landing page: **/truck-driver-health-insurance**
- A **Refer and Earn** program that can pay partners for referrals
- A **Business Leads** board in your admin portal to track every trucking company you are working
- A licensed brokerage in 40+ states, so you can cover drivers wherever they run

## 2. Who You Are Targeting

Work these in order of how easy they are to reach and how many drivers they touch.

**Tier 1: Direct fleet and driver access**
- Owner-operator trucking companies (small and mid-size fleets, 5 to 100 trucks)
- Lease-purchase and lease-operator programs (their drivers are 1099 and need coverage badly)
- Logistics and freight companies that use owner-operators
- Dispatch companies (they talk to dozens of independent drivers daily)

**Tier 2: Hubs that reach many drivers at once**
- Factoring companies (they handle drivers' money and are trusted advisors)
- CDL schools and driver training programs (new drivers with no coverage)
- Truck stops and driver service centers
- ELD and trucking software providers (they have driver customer bases)
- Trucking and owner-operator associations (OOIDA-type groups, state trucking associations)

**Tier 3: Community and content**
- Trucking podcasts, YouTube channels, and Facebook groups for owner-operators
- Local truck maintenance shops and truck insurance agents (non-competing, they insure the truck, you insure the driver)

The best partners are the ones who already have the driver's trust and touch them regularly: dispatch, factoring, and lease programs.

## 3. Your Offer: Three Partnership Models

Pick the model that fits each partner. Lead with Model A because it is the easiest yes.

**Model A: Free Value-Add Perk (easiest to close)**
- You become the company's recommended health insurance resource, free to the company.
- The company simply tells drivers "we partner with VS Health Benefits for health coverage, reach out to them."
- No cost, no work for the company. They just look good to their drivers.
- Best for: companies that will not take a payment or want to keep it simple.

**Model B: Paid Referral Partner (your Refer and Earn program)**
- The company or its owner joins your Refer and Earn program and earns a payout for every driver who enrolls.
- Turns your program into a revenue stream for the partner, which motivates them to actually send drivers.
- Best for: dispatch companies, factoring companies, and owners who want a piece of the upside.
- Note: partners must be VS Health Benefits clients to collect payouts, which is a soft way to also insure the owner.

**Model C: Embedded Benefits Partner (biggest, longest to build)**
- You become the official benefits provider a fleet points every new driver to during onboarding.
- Co-branded page or link, a spot in their driver welcome packet, and a standing referral flow.
- Best for: mid-size fleets and lease programs onboarding drivers regularly.

You can offer all three and let the partner choose. Most will start at A or B and grow into C.

## 4. The Value Proposition (what is in it for them)

Frame every conversation around the partner's interest, not yours.

**For the company or fleet:**
- Helps recruit and retain drivers, which is their number one pain
- Costs them nothing and takes them no work
- Makes them look like they invest in their people
- Optional income if they join Refer and Earn

**For the driver (which the partner cares about):**
- Free, no-pressure help from a licensed advisor who understands 1099 truckers
- Coverage that travels with them across state lines
- They may deduct 100% of premiums as a self-employed driver
- ACA subsidies checked so they only pay what they should

Your one-liner: "I help your drivers get real health coverage built for truckers, at no cost to you, and it helps you keep them."

## 5. The Pitch

**Elevator pitch (15 seconds):**
"I run VS Health Benefits, a licensed brokerage that specializes in health insurance for truckers. I partner with fleets and dispatch companies to give their drivers free help getting covered. It costs you nothing, your drivers get taken care of, and it helps you keep them on the road."

**The hook that opens doors:**
Lead with driver retention, not insurance. Owners do not want another vendor pitch, but they always want to keep drivers. "Losing drivers is expensive. One thing that helps keep them is benefits, and I make that free and easy for you."

**Proof points to have ready:**
- Licensed in 40+ states
- Specialize in 1099 and owner-operator drivers
- 100% free service, paid by the carriers not the driver
- The tax write-off angle (deduct 100% of premiums)
- Your truck-driver page as a leave-behind link

## 6. Outreach Plan

**Step 1: Build your target list (Week 1)**
Use your admin Business Leads board. Pull 50 to 100 target companies:
- Search FMCSA / SAFER for carriers with owner-operators in your states
- Local and regional fleets, dispatch services, factoring companies
- LinkedIn for owners, safety managers, and driver recruiters at logistics companies

**Step 2: Warm up with content (ongoing)**
- Post on LinkedIn and in trucking Facebook groups about "health insurance for owner-operators"
- Share the truck-driver page and the tax write-off angle
- This makes you a known name before you reach out cold

**Step 3: Multi-touch outreach (Weeks 1 to 8)**
For each target, run a simple sequence:
1. Connect on LinkedIn with a short, driver-retention-focused note
2. Send a short email or message with your one-liner and the truck-driver page link
3. Call or text the owner or recruiter (truckers and fleet owners respond to phone and text)
4. Offer a 10-minute call, not a sales meeting
5. Follow up 3 to 4 times over two weeks, then move on if no response

**Step 4: Ask for the referral flow, not a signature**
Your ask is simple: "Can I be the person you point your drivers to when they need health insurance? I will make it dead simple." Get a yes, then set up how drivers reach you.

## 7. Partner Onboarding (once they say yes)

Make it effortless for the partner:

1. Give them a simple way to send drivers to you: a phone number, a text keyword, and the truck-driver page link.
2. If they join Refer and Earn, set up their account so they can submit driver referrals and track payouts.
3. Give them a one-pager or flyer to hand to drivers or drop in onboarding packets.
4. For embedded partners, add a co-branded note or link to their driver welcome materials.
5. Confirm how you will report back to them (which drivers enrolled, so they see the value).

Then deliver fast. When a driver reaches out, respond same day. Your speed and care are what make the partner keep sending drivers.

## 9. Tracking and Managing

Use the Business Leads board in your admin portal as your partner CRM:

- Ask partners to introduce you to associations and larger fleets
- Consider a simple referral-partner agreement for the serious ones
- Goal: 15 to 20 partners, a steady flow of driver referrals

## 12. Objection Handling

**"We do not want to deal with insurance."**
"You will not have to. You just point drivers to me and I handle everything. It is free and takes you zero work."

**"Our drivers are 1099, that is on them."**
"Exactly, and that is why they need help finding coverage. When you point them to me, it looks like you care, which helps you keep them, and it costs you nothing."

**"We already have someone."**
"No problem. Keep me as a backup for drivers who are not getting the help they need, and I will make it easy for the ones who ask."

**"What is the catch or cost?"**
"There is none for you. I am paid by the insurance carriers, not by you or your drivers. If you want, you can even earn a referral payout when a driver enrolls."

## The One Thing to Remember

Sell driver retention, not insurance. Every fleet and logistics owner wants to keep drivers and spends nothing to do it. You give them a free way to look like they care while you quietly build a pipeline of 1099 drivers who need exactly what you offer. Start with dispatch and factoring companies, deliver fast, and let your best partners introduce you to the rest.
