If you want coverage in place for January 1

Last note from me on this one.

Group coverage has no national deadline. You can start a plan in any month. But January 1 is the effective date most employers want, because the plan year then lines up with the tax year and your employees' deductibles reset when the calendar does. That date has a queue in front of it.

What a January 1 start actually takes

Quotes and side by side comparisona few days
Your decision and employee enrollmentone to two weeks
Carrier submission and approvaltwo to three weeks out

Which is why the useful move is to start the quote now and decide later, rather than decide now and start the quote in December.

One timing note if your employees currently buy their own coverage. Individual open enrollment runs November 1 to January 15, and they need to be enrolled by December 15 for a January 1 start. That matters if an ICHRA or QSEHRA turns out to be the right fit for you.
