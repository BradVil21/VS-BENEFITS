Four ways to cover a team. Most owners know only one.

Following up on my note about covering your team. If it is on the list for this year, the piece that decides your cost is which structure you use. There are four, and most owners only know the first one.

1
Traditional small group plan
One plan you choose, with the premium split between you and your employees. Starts at 2 enrolled lives and is available year round.
2
Level funded plan
Group coverage with a refund built in. If your team stays healthy, money can come back to you at the end of the plan year. Often the strongest value for a younger or healthier crew.
3
ICHRA
Instead of buying one plan, you give each employee a tax free monthly allowance and they choose their own individual plan. You set the budget. They choose the coverage.
4
QSEHRA
The simpler version of that for teams under 50. Capped amounts, lighter rules, and no group plan to administer.
 

The same eight person shop can see very different numbers across those four. Which one wins comes down to your headcount, the ages on your team, your monthly budget, and how much paperwork you are willing to touch.
