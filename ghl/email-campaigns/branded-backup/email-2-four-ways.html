Last week I mentioned that offering benefits is more doable than most owners expect. Here is the part that actually decides your cost — which of the four structures you use.

1. Traditional small group plan
One plan, you pick the carrier and level, you split the premium with your employees. Works from 2 enrolled lives and can start any month of the year.
 
2. Level-funded plan
Group coverage with a refund mechanic: if your team stays healthy, you can get money back at year end. Often the best value for younger or healthier crews.
 
3. ICHRA
Instead of buying one plan, you give each employee a tax-free monthly allowance and they choose their own individual plan. Your budget is a number you set. Their plan choice is theirs.
 
4. QSEHRA
The under-50-employee version of reimbursement. Simpler rules, capped amounts, no group plan to administer. Good for very small teams that want to help without running one.

Which one wins comes down to four things: team size, ages, your monthly budget, and how much administration you are willing to touch. The same 8-person shop can see meaningfully different numbers across these four.
