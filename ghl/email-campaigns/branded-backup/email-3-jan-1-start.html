Last note from me on this, and it is the practical one.

Small group coverage does not have a national deadline — you can start a group plan in any month. But a January 1 effective date is the one most employers want, because it lines the plan year up with the calendar year, the tax year, and your employees' deductibles. And that date does have a queue in front of it.

What a Jan 1 start actually takes

Quotes and plan comparison — a few days
Your decision and employee enrollment — 1 to 2 weeks
Carrier submission and approval — typically 2 to 3 weeks before the effective date

Which is why the useful move is to start the quote now and decide later, rather than decide now and start the quote in December.

If your employees are currently buying their own coverage, note that the individual open enrollment window runs November 1 to January 15, and they need to be enrolled by December 15 for a January 1 start. That timing matters if an ICHRA or QSEHRA ends up being the right structure for you.
