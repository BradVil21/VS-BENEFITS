Quick Answer

When Is Open Enrollment for 2027?

For 2027 coverage, open enrollment on the federal marketplace and in most states, including Florida, runs from November 1 to December 15. This is shorter than past years. Enroll by December 15 and your plan starts January 1.

  • December 15: Last day to enroll for a January 1 start

Common Questions

Open Enrollment, Deadlines, and Special Enrollment

Clear answers to the questions people ask most about enrolling in individual and family health insurance.

Dates and Deadlines

When is open enrollment for 2027 health insurance?

For 2027 coverage, open enrollment runs November 1 through December 15 on the federal marketplace and in most states, including Florida. Enroll by December 15 for coverage that starts January 1.

Why is the open enrollment window shorter this year?

A federal rule shortened the open enrollment period starting with 2027 coverage. In past years many states allowed enrollment through January 15. Now most end on December 15, so it is important not to wait.

When does my coverage start if I enroll during open enrollment?

If you enroll and pay your first premium by December 15, your coverage starts January 1. A few state based marketplaces may allow a slightly later start, but the federal deadline is December 15.

Do I have to re-enroll every year?

Most plans renew automatically, but you should review your coverage every year. Prices, networks, and subsidy amounts change, and the plan that fit last year may not be the best value this year.

Missing Open Enrollment

What happens if I miss open enrollment?

If you miss the December 15 deadline and do not qualify for a special enrollment period, you generally have to wait until the next open enrollment to get an individual plan. That is why it helps to start early.

Can I get health insurance outside of open enrollment?

Yes, in several ways. You can enroll any time if you have a qualifying life event, if you are eligible for Medicaid or CHIP, or through certain other coverage options. A broker can tell you what you qualify for in minutes.

What is a Special Enrollment Period?

A special enrollment period is a 60 day window that opens when you have a qualifying life event. During it you can enroll in or change a plan outside the normal open enrollment dates.

What counts as a qualifying life event?

Common examples include losing job based or other coverage, getting married or divorced, having or adopting a baby, moving to a new area, or a change in income that affects your subsidy eligibility.

Subsidies and Choosing a Plan

Are subsidies still available for 2027 plans?

The original ACA premium tax credit still exists, so many people still get help paying their premium. The extra enhanced subsidies that ran from 2021 through 2025 have expired, so some households now pay more. We check exactly what you qualify for at no cost.

Can I switch plans during open enrollment?

Yes. Open enrollment is the time to change your metal tier, switch carriers, add or drop dependents, or move to a plan with a better network or lower cost. We compare your options side by side.

Should I pick the cheapest plan?

Not always. The lowest premium can come with a high deductible that costs more if you use care. The right choice balances your monthly premium against how much you expect to use your coverage. We help you weigh both.

How do I enroll and does help cost anything?

You can enroll on the marketplace yourself or let a licensed broker do it for you. Our help is completely free because we are paid by the carriers, not by you. We handle the paperwork and answer your questions.
