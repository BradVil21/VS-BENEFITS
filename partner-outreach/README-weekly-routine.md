# Partner referrals: weekly routine (Nov 11, 2026)

Goal: 10–15 partners who each send 1–3 drivers a month, and links from trucking sites so
/truck-driver-health-insurance climbs from page 3–4 (pos ~37) onto page 1.
Full strategy is in `trucking-partner-game-plan.md`. This is the doing part.

Target list: `partner-targets.csv` (25 real businesses, grouped into Weeks 1–5).
Track status there or move them onto the Business Leads board in admin.

## The routine: 2 hours a week, Tuesday mornings

| Time | Do this |
|---|---|
| 30 min | Contact this week's 5 targets (call first, then send the follow-up email the same day) |
| 30 min | Follow up with last week's 5 (one text or email, then a call if no answer) |
| 30 min | One drop-in visit: a DOT clinic or CDL school with flyers + QR card |
| 30 min | One link ask or guest-post pitch (Week 4–5 rows), and update the CSV |

Rule: every partner gets **one** ask: "Can I be the person you send drivers to for health
insurance?" Then make it easy with a QR card, a short link and a referral payout.

Short link to hand out: `vshealthbenefits.com/trucking-health-quote`
(Add `?ref=PARTNERNAME` so you can see who sent the lead in the form's origin panel.)

## Call script: DOT clinic / CDL school (60 seconds)

> Hi, this is Bradley with VS Health Benefits. I'm a licensed health insurance broker here in South
> Florida, and I work with a lot of truck drivers. Your drivers ask about health insurance, right?
> Most of them don't have it or overpay. I'd love to drop off a small stack of cards so you can hand
> them off. It's free for the driver and I'm happy to pay a referral thank-you. Who should I talk to
> about putting a few at the front desk?

For CDL schools add: *"New grads usually wait 60–90 days for company benefits. I help them cover
that gap. Could I do a 10-minute talk for a class, or get a card into the graduation packet?"*
