Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a small dental office offer group health insurance?

Yes. Practices with as few as two employees can qualify for group or level funded coverage. We match the plan to your team size and budget.

How many employees does a dental practice need for a group plan?

Most group plans require at least two enrolled employees, which covers the great majority of independent practices. ICHRA can work for very small or solo setups.

Can I bundle medical with dental and vision for my staff?

Yes, and most practices do. Bundling medical, dental, vision, and life into one package is simple to administer and strengthens your recruiting offer.

Is group health insurance tax deductible for my practice?

Employer contributions to employee premiums are generally tax deductible, and some small practices qualify for the Small Business Health Care Tax Credit. Confirm specifics with your accountant.

How much does dental office health insurance cost?

Single coverage commonly runs about 350 to 650 dollars per month, usually shared with the employee. Level funded plans can cost less for a healthy team. We show exact numbers free.
