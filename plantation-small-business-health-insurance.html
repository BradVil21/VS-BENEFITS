What makes Plantation different when you buy group coverage

We write group plans for Plantation employers in corporate and back-office employers in Plantation Midtown, medical and dental practices, legal and accounting firms, IT and telecom, insurance and finance offices, and home services. The businesses here have a specific set of problems, and a broker who has never worked a Broward renewal will miss all four of these.

Your employees benchmark against corporate plans.

Plantation Midtown puts your 12-person firm next door to companies with full HR departments. You don't need their budget to compete — you need a plan that looks credible on paper: a recognizable carrier, a real PPO option, and dental and vision attached. We build that package for what most owners assume buys far less.

Cleveland Clinic Weston access is a genuine differentiator.

Broward employees increasingly want Cleveland Clinic Florida in network. Participation varies meaningfully by carrier and plan tier, and it's the single most common thing we get asked to verify for Plantation groups. We check it before you see the quote.

Professional groups are prime candidates for level-funded plans.

Office-based employers in the 15-60 range tend to have favorable claims experience. Level-funded plans price on your actual group instead of the community pool and refund unused claims dollars — often 10-25% below the fully insured quote. We run both so you can see it rather than take our word for it.

Section 125 and HSA setup is free money most owners skip.

A premium-only Section 125 plan lets employees pay their share pre-tax, which cuts the company's payroll tax bill too. Pair it with an HSA-qualified option and the tax savings alone often cover a meaningful slice of your contribution. It takes us about a week to implement.

Straight answers for Plantation owners

Frequently asked

How many employees do I need for a group health plan in Plantation?

Two. Most carriers writing business in Broward County issue small-group health plans starting at 2 enrolled employees, and the owner can count as one of them. If your Plantation team is smaller than that or is mostly 1099, we build an ACA or individual strategy instead so nobody goes without coverage.

How much does small business health insurance cost in Plantation, FL?

Most Broward small groups land between roughly $360 and $670 per employee per month, driven by plan tier, network breadth and the ages on your team. You decide how much of that the business pays — many Plantation employers cover 50 to 100 percent of the employee-only premium and let staff buy up for dependents. Businesses under 25 employees with lower average wages may also qualify for the Small Business Health Care Tax Credit, worth up to 50 percent of what you contribute. A free quote gives you your actual number in about a day.

What does it cost to work with VS Health Benefits?

Nothing. We are an independent advisor compensated by the carrier when you enroll — the same premium you would pay going direct. Quotes, plan comparisons, enrollment meetings, paperwork, ID cards, claims help and renewals are all free to your Plantation business.

Which hospitals and doctors will my Plantation employees have access to?

That depends entirely on the network you choose, which is why we check it first. Your team will likely want HCA Florida Westside Hospital, Plantation General and Cleveland Clinic Weston nearby in network. We verify participation for those specific facilities on every plan before we present it to you, rather than after you have signed.

Can I cover myself as the owner and my 1099 contractors too?

Owners can go on the group plan. 1099 contractors generally cannot, but they can often do better on an ACA marketplace plan with a subsidy review — sometimes at a lower net cost than the group. Plenty of Plantation businesses run exactly this split, and we set both sides up in the same conversation.

When can I start a group plan — do I have to wait for Open Enrollment?

No. Group health plans can start any month of the year in Plantation. The November 1 to January 15 Open Enrollment window applies to individual and ACA marketplace plans, not to employer group coverage. If you want a January 1 effective date, we should be talking by mid-November.

How is this different from calling a carrier directly?

Calling Florida Blue gets you Florida Blue's plans. We quote every major carrier writing in Broward County side by side, compare the networks against where your employees actually live, and build the combination that fits your budget. Same price, more options, and someone in your corner at renewal.

How fast can coverage start for my Plantation business?

Most Plantation small groups get a full comparison within one business day of sending a simple employee census, and coverage typically starts on the first of the following month. We handle the carrier paperwork, employee enrollment meetings and ID card distribution so you do not lose a workday to it.
