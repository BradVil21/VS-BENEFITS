Why Health Insurance Costs Less Than You Think

Most people who skip coverage never saw their subsidized price. Marketplace tax credits are based on your household income, and they scale: the less you earn, the more the government pays toward your premium. Depending on income and household size, a plan with a several hundred dollar sticker price can cost a fraction of that out of pocket. The catch is that nobody mails you this number. You have to run it, or have someone run it for you. That is what we do, free, in about five minutes.

Your ZIP Code Sets Your Menu and Your Price

Carriers price by county, and carrier lineups change from one rating area to the next. The same silver plan can differ in price between neighboring counties, and some carriers only sell in certain areas. This is why national averages are useless for your decision. The only numbers that matter are the ones for your ZIP code, your age, and your income.

The Four Paths to Affordable Coverage

  • Subsidized marketplace plans. The workhorse. Income based tax credits applied monthly to a real ACA plan with full protections. Our guide to what income counts for subsidies shows how the math works.
  • Medicaid and CHIP. Free or near free if your income qualifies, and you can enroll any month of the year.
  • Catastrophic plans for under 30. Low premiums that protect against worst case bills.
  • Right sized plan tiers. Sometimes affordable means not overbuying. A bronze plan with a subsidy can beat paying cash for a gold plan you do not need. See how the tiers compare in our bronze vs silver vs gold guide.

What People Actually Pay

Real premiums depend on age, state, income, and tier. Without any subsidy, individual plans commonly run several hundred dollars a month. With subsidies, many households pay far less, and some pay very little at all. Our full breakdown of health insurance costs has the ranges. Your number takes one ZIP code and one phone call.

Affordable Health Insurance FAQ

How can I find affordable health insurance?

Start with your ZIP code and your income. Plans and prices vary by county, and income based subsidies often cut the monthly cost dramatically. We compare every carrier in your area for free and apply every subsidy you qualify for.

What is the cheapest health insurance option?

For most people it is a subsidized marketplace plan. Medicaid is free or near free for those who qualify. The cheapest plan is not always the best value, which is why a side by side comparison matters.

Is there really free help to find a plan?

Yes. Licensed advisors are paid by the carriers, not by you. Your premium is identical either way, you just get expert help at no cost.

Why does my ZIP code change my price?

Carriers set rates by county and rating area, and not every carrier sells in every county. Any real comparison starts with where you live.

Can I get affordable coverage right now?

During open enrollment, yes. Outside it you need a qualifying life event, and Medicaid enrolls year round. One quick call settles which applies to you.
