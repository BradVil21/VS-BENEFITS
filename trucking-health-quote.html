How truck driver health insurance quotes work

  1. Enter your home ZIP

    Health plans are priced by where you live, not where you park the truck. Your home ZIP decides which plans and prices you can get.

  2. Answer a few quick questions

    Who needs coverage and their ages, whether anyone uses tobacco, and your estimated household income. For owner-operators that is net profit after truck expenses, not gross revenue.

  3. A licensed advisor checks your quote

    We compare plans side by side, check that your doctors and prescriptions are covered, and look for networks that work across state lines. We reach out within 1 business day, often within hours.

What sets the price of your quote

  • Home ZIP and age. Under ACA rules these two drive most of the price. Your health history does not.
  • Income. Marketplace tax credits are based on household income. Above 400% of the poverty level (about $63,840 for a single driver on 2027 coverage) most people lose the credit, so a good mileage year can change your price. Try the owner-operator subsidy cliff calculator.
  • Network. A nationwide PPO usually costs more than a local HMO, but it lets you see doctors in other states.
  • Plan level and tobacco. Bronze, silver and gold trade a lower premium for a higher deductible, and tobacco use can add a surcharge.

Truck driver quote FAQ

How long does a truck driver health insurance quote take?

The online form takes about a minute. A licensed advisor follows up within 1 business day, often within hours, by call or text, whichever works from the road.

Does a quote cost anything or commit me to a plan?

No. Quotes are free and there is no obligation. Insurance companies pay us, and you pay the same premium you would pay buying the plan yourself.

Can I get a quote outside open enrollment?

Yes. For 2027 coverage, open enrollment runs November 1 to January 15 in most states, and you need to enroll by December 15 for coverage that starts January 1. Outside that window you need a qualifying life event, such as losing coverage when you leave a carrier. See the 2027 open enrollment guide for drivers.

Will my plan work when I'm on the road in another state?

Emergency care is covered at in-network cost sharing anywhere in the U.S. under ACA rules. Routine care depends on the network, so we look for plans with national PPO networks when you run out of state. More in using your plan out of state.

Does health insurance cover my DOT physical?

Usually not, because a DOT physical is a work requirement rather than preventive care. There are ways to pay less. See does insurance cover a DOT physical.
