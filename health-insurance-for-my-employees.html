First: Are You Even Required To?

If you have fewer than 50 full time equivalent employees, no. The ACA employer mandate only applies at 50 and up, which means the overwhelming majority of small businesses offer coverage because they want to, not because they must. And the reasons they want to are practical: the best workers pick jobs with benefits, they stay longer once they have them, and replacing a trained employee usually costs more than insuring one. Our guide on benefits as a hiring and retention tool covers that math.

Your Four Ways to Cover a Team

  • Traditional small group plan. The classic: one plan, you pick the carrier and level, you share the premium with employees. Works from 2 enrolled lives, available year round.
  • Level funded plan. Group coverage with a twist: if your team stays healthy, you can get money back at year end. Often the best value for younger or healthier teams.
  • ICHRA. Instead of one plan, you give each employee a tax free monthly allowance and they pick their own individual plan. Your budget is fixed, their choice is theirs.
  • QSEHRA. The under 50 employee version of reimbursement, simpler rules, capped amounts. Good for very small teams that want to help without running a group plan.

Which one wins depends on your team size, ages, budget, and how much administration you want. That comparison is exactly what we do, free.

What It Actually Costs

Small group premiums typically land in the few hundred dollars per employee per month range before your contribution split. Most employers pay half or more of the employee-only premium and let employees buy up for family coverage. Two things soften the number: every dollar you contribute is a deductible business expense, and businesses under 25 employees with modest average wages may qualify for the Small Business Health Care Tax Credit on top. Offering Employee Health Insurance FAQ

Am I required to offer health insurance to my employees?

Only at 50 or more full time equivalent employees. Below that it is optional, and it is the benefit employees value most.

How much does it cost to offer health insurance?

Typically a few hundred dollars per employee per month before your contribution split, and your share is tax deductible. Exact pricing depends on your state, team ages, and plan level.

What are my options besides a traditional group plan?

Level funded plans, ICHRA, and QSEHRA reimbursement arrangements. We price all of them side by side.

Is there a tax credit?

Eligible businesses under 25 full time equivalent employees with modest average wages may qualify for the Small Business Health Care Tax Credit, and premiums are generally deductible either way.

How many employees do I need?

Usually just two enrolled, and you can start a group plan any month of the year.
