The 1099 Reality

What Every Independent Contractor Needs to Know About Health Insurance

When you work for yourself, there is no employer covering part of your premium. But there are real options that make coverage affordable, and even tax advantages that employees do not get.

  • Self-employed workers can deduct 100% of premiums on their taxes
  • ACA marketplace plans offer income-based subsidies that cut your cost
  • Your net income after deductions determines your subsidy amount
  • PPO plans let you see any specialist without a referral
  • Coverage can start as fast as the 1st of next month
  • You are not tied to your employer, so your coverage moves with you

Common Questions

1099 Health Insurance FAQ

Can 1099 workers get health insurance?

Yes. 1099 contractors and freelancers can access the same ACA marketplace plans, private PPO plans, and individual market products as anyone else. We help you compare them all at no cost.

Is health insurance tax deductible for the self-employed?

Self-employed individuals who pay their own premiums may be able to deduct 100% of their health insurance costs on their federal tax return. This is a major financial advantage compared to being a traditional employee. Ask your tax advisor for your specific situation.

Do I qualify for ACA subsidies as a freelancer?

Many 1099 workers qualify for premium tax credits on the ACA marketplace. Your eligibility is based on your estimated net income for the year. We calculate your subsidy before comparing plans so you know exactly what you will pay.

What happens if my income changes throughout the year?

You can update your income estimate anytime on the ACA marketplace to adjust your subsidies. We help you set the right estimate upfront to avoid a surprise at tax time.

Can I keep my same doctors?

We check provider networks before recommending any plan. If keeping your current doctors is a priority, we confirm they are in-network before you enroll.
