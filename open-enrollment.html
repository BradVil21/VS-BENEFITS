What Is Open Enrollment & When Can You Sign Up?

Open Enrollment is the one time each year when anyone can enroll in a new health insurance plan or switch their current plan for any reason, no qualifying event required. For 2027 coverage, the federal Health Insurance Marketplace Open Enrollment Period runs from November 1, 2026 through January 15, 2027. If you enroll by December 15, 2026, your coverage starts January 1, 2027. Enroll between December 16 and January 15 and your coverage generally starts February 1, 2027.

Missing the window usually means waiting a full year, unless you qualify for a Special Enrollment Period. That's why thousands of people add their name early: so a licensed broker can reach out the moment enrollment opens and help them lock in the right plan and the biggest subsidy they qualify for, at no cost.

Key 2027 dates

Nov 1, 2026, Open Enrollment begins.
Dec 15, 2026, Deadline for Jan 1 coverage.

FAQ

Open Enrollment questions, answered

When is Open Enrollment for 2027 health insurance?

The 2027 Open Enrollment Period runs from November 1, 2026 through January 15, 2027 on the federal Marketplace. Enroll by December 15, 2026 for coverage that starts January 1, 2027. Plans selected after December 15 generally start February 1, 2027.

How much does it cost to use a broker?

Nothing. A licensed independent broker is paid a commission by the insurance carrier, and plan prices are set by law, so a plan never costs more when you enroll through a broker. Our help is 100% free with no obligation.

Can I switch my current plan during open enrollment?

Yes. Open Enrollment is the time each year when anyone can enroll in a new plan or switch plans for any reason. We compare your options side by side so you keep your doctors and lower your costs where possible.

What if I miss the deadline?

If you miss Open Enrollment you may still qualify for a Special Enrollment Period after a qualifying life event such as losing coverage, moving, marriage, or a new baby. Add your details and we'll tell you if you qualify.

Do you help small businesses with group open enrollment?

Yes. We help small businesses (typically 2 to 50 employees) shop and set up group health coverage, compare carriers, and handle enrollment for their team. Choose the business option on the form and we'll reach out.
