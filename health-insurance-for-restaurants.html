Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a small restaurant offer health insurance?

Yes. Restaurants with as few as two employees can qualify for group or level funded coverage. Level funded plans are often the most affordable route for a healthy team.

Do I have to cover part-time or tipped employees?

Generally you offer coverage to full-time staff, defined as 30 or more hours per week on average. We help you track hours and decide who is eligible without overcommitting.

How much does restaurant health insurance cost?

Single coverage commonly runs about 350 to 650 dollars per month and is usually split with the employee. Level funded options can lower that for a healthy team. We show exact numbers free.

What is a level funded plan and is it good for restaurants?

A level funded plan has a fixed monthly cost and can return money if your team stays healthy. For younger, healthier restaurant teams it is often the best value.

When can a restaurant enroll in group health insurance?

Group coverage can be set up any time of year. There is no open enrollment window to wait for like there is with individual plans.
