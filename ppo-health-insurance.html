What is a PPO health plan and how does it work?

A PPO, or preferred provider organization, lets you see any doctor or specialist without a referral and pays a share of out-of-network care. That is the trade: more freedom than an HMO or EPO, and usually a higher monthly premium. A PPO is worth the difference if you travel, split time between states, already have specialists you want to keep, or live somewhere the HMO networks are thin.

PPO Health Insurance: What You Need to Know

PPO plans in Florida vary widely by carrier and county. Here is what matters most when comparing private PPO plans in the Florida market.

Network Size Varies by County

In Miami-Dade and Broward, some carriers have narrow networks that exclude major hospital systems like Baptist Health or Jackson Health. PPO plans typically give you out-of-network access as a safety net, but checking your specific doctors before enrolling is critical.

ACA PPO Plans vs Private PPO Plans

Some ACA marketplace plans in Florida offer PPO-style networks and may come with premium tax credits if your income qualifies. Private off-marketplace PPO plans are available year-round and typically offer broader networks -- but without subsidies. We compare both for every client.

PPO Carriers We Compare

Florida Blue (Blue Cross Blue Shield of Florida) has the largest statewide PPO network in Florida. UnitedHealthcare and Aetna offer strong national PPO networks ideal for frequent travelers and self-employed workers with clients across multiple states. We compare all three side by side.

PPO Plans for Self-Employed Floridians

Owner-operators, 1099 contractors, and self-employed professionals in Florida are among the most common PPO buyers because they want complete flexibility without a gatekeeper. If you are self-employed, your PPO premium may also be 100% tax deductible as a business expense.

PPO Health Insurance FAQ

What is a PPO health insurance plan?+

A PPO (Preferred Provider Organization) is a type of health insurance plan that lets you see any doctor or specialist without a referral. You have a network of in-network providers with lower costs, but you can also use out-of-network providers at a higher cost-sharing level. PPOs are the most flexible type of health insurance plan and are popular with self-employed workers, frequent travelers, and anyone who wants full control over their care.

What is the difference between a PPO and an HMO?+

The main difference is flexibility. With a PPO, you can see any doctor -- in-network or out-of-network -- without a referral and without choosing a primary care doctor. With an HMO, you must choose a primary care physician who coordinates all your care, and you generally need a referral to see a specialist. HMOs typically have lower premiums but much more restricted access. PPOs cost more but give you complete control over who you see and when.

Are PPO plans available in Florida?+

Yes. PPO plans are available throughout Florida through carriers including Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, Aetna, and Cigna. Availability varies by county -- some rural counties have fewer PPO options than Miami-Dade or Broward. VS Health Benefits checks PPO availability specifically in your zip code and compares networks so you know exactly which doctors and hospitals are covered.

How much does a PPO health insurance plan cost in Florida?+

PPO premiums in Florida vary based on your age, zip code, tobacco use, and plan tier. A single adult can expect to pay roughly $350 to $650 per month for a private PPO plan before any ACA subsidies. If your income qualifies for ACA premium tax credits, a PPO-style marketplace plan may cost significantly less. We run exact quotes for your situation at no cost.

Can self-employed workers get a PPO plan in Florida?+

Yes. Self-employed workers, 1099 contractors, owner-operators, and freelancers in Florida can all purchase individual PPO plans. If your net income qualifies, you may also be eligible for ACA premium tax credits that significantly reduce the monthly cost of a PPO plan. The self-employed health insurance deduction may also allow you to deduct 100% of your premiums from taxable income.

What is the best PPO health insurance in Florida?+

Florida Blue has the widest statewide PPO network in Florida and is a strong choice for people who see local providers. UnitedHealthcare and Aetna are excellent for people who travel frequently or need multi-state network access -- particularly useful for truck drivers and owner-operators. The best choice depends on your specific doctors, location, and budget. We compare all carriers side by side for free.

Is a PPO plan worth it in Florida?+

For many people in Florida, yes. If you have preferred doctors you do not want to give up, travel frequently, are self-employed and want maximum flexibility, or have been burned by narrow-network HMO plans in the past -- a PPO is usually worth the higher premium. The key is finding a PPO with a competitive rate for your zip code, which is exactly what VS Health Benefits does.
