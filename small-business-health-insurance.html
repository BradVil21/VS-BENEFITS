Common Questions

Small Business Health Insurance FAQ

Can a small business with 2 employees offer group health insurance?

Yes. Businesses with as few as 2 employees can qualify for micro-group plans. VS Benefits specializes in coverage for 2 to 25 employees and handles all setup at no cost to you.

When can a small business enroll in group health insurance?

Group health insurance can be set up year-round. Unlike individual ACA plans, there is no open enrollment window to wait for. You can start coverage for your team any month of the year.

Is group health insurance a tax deduction for my business?

Yes. Employer contributions to employee health premiums are generally tax-deductible as a business expense. Small employers may also qualify for the Small Business Health Care Tax Credit. Consult your tax advisor for specifics.

Do I have to contribute to my employees' premiums?

Most carriers require employers to cover at least 50% of employee-only premiums. VS Benefits shows you the minimum required and helps you structure a competitive package within your budget.

What happens when I hire a new employee?

New employees typically have a qualifying event window to enroll when hired. We walk you through adds, drops, and life event changes throughout the year as part of ongoing support.
