90 days ago, Google barely knew my business existed.

Last week, it showed my website more than 6,000 times.

I run a health insurance agency in South Florida. No marketing team. No agency on retainer. Most days I'm still on the phones.

So I built the machine at night.

Here's what 90 days of that looks like:

📈 2.4x more clicks from Google (66 → 156 a month)
🔎 Average ranking went from #57 to #31, and #23 last week
📊 Weekly clicks went from 2 to 46 at peak
🧮 A free ACA subsidy calculator I built brought in 74 clicks on its own
🏆 Search "VS Health Benefits" and we're #1

Behind the scenes:

🤖 An AI phone agent that answers 24/7
🧲 Every lead from the site, chat, and referrals lands in one CRM
📞 One click on a lead and my dialer is already calling them
🗺️ Licensed in 40+ states, with pages built for truckers, owner-operators, and small businesses

The biggest lesson surprised me.

More pages didn't win. Better answers did.

I merged 11 duplicate pages, and the ones left started ranking higher. I put the real dates and real prices in page titles. I stopped writing what I wanted to say and started answering what people type into Google at 11pm.

"When is open enrollment 2027?"
"Can owner-operators deduct health insurance?"
"How much does it cost to cover my employees?"

Is it done? Not even close. Most of my income still comes from the phone.

But every month, a little more of it comes from something I own.

Next goal: page 1 on Google for our 2027 Open Enrollment guide before November 1.
