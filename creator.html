How rewards work

  1. Quote requested: someone used your link and asked us for a quote.
  2. Qualified: they finished a free consultation with one of our licensed advisors. That earns your reward, whether or not they buy.
  3. Paid: we sent your reward. You'll get a text and email each step of the way.

For their privacy, we never share who your referrals are or anything about their coverage.

Rewards are a flat amount per qualified referral and are never tied to whether anyone buys insurance (s. 626.112(8), Florida Statutes). Where state law does not allow referral rewards, we cannot pay one. VS BENEFITS LLC is a licensed independent insurance agency, NPN 22116298.
