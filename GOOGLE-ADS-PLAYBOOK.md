# Google Ads playbook (Bradley's standing rule, 2026-11-25)

**Rule:** any time Bradley asks for anything Google Ads (a campaign, ad copy, keywords,
a review, budget changes), build it from this playbook. Source: Sabri Suby, "6 Google Ads
hacks" (youtube.com/watch?v=7xtRHa7b96w), adapted for a licensed health insurance agency.
Campaign files that already exist: GOOGLE-ADS-trucker-campaign.md,
GOOGLE-ADS-smallbusiness-campaign.md, GOOGLE-ADS-dental-vision-test.md.

## 1. Coliseum keywords (80/20, and 4% -> 64%)
- About 20% of keywords bring about 80% of leads. About 4% bring about 64%. Find those 4-5 keywords and build around them.
- Judge a keyword by **buyer intent and money made**, not by search volume or CPC.
  Ask: "Would someone typing this want to buy a plan from us?"
- Process: export keywords and search terms (cost, clicks, conversions, cost per lead,
  sales if known). Claude ranks them and finds the few that drive most results.
- Likely VS coliseum keywords (confirm with data): "health insurance for truck drivers",
  "owner operator health insurance", "small business health insurance [state]",
  "self employed health insurance", "health insurance for [trade] companies".

## 2. Sell the click, not the product
- The ad only has to earn the click. Searchers want answers, so promise the answer.
- Use the keyword, then add a benefit plus curiosity. Don't write a plain "Keyword + Company" ad like everyone else.
- **Halo research before writing:** read Reddit (r/Truckers, r/smallbusiness), Facebook groups,
  Quora and reviews for the buyer's hopes, fears, pains and dreams. Use their own words.
- Write many headlines, then cut to the best 15 (RSA limit).
- **Compliance comes first (insurance):** curiosity is fine, but claims must be true. No guaranteed prices,
  no "free insurance", no implying government affiliation, nothing misleading (Fla. Stat. 626.9541
  and state unfair-trade rules). Keep Bradley's licensed-agent wording. Example: "What OTR Drivers Wish They
  Knew About Coverage" or "7 Mistakes Owner-Operators Make on Health Plans". Avoid a made-up "secret".

## 3. Knife to the neck (bonsai tree), not the fishing trawler
- Start with **only** high-intent keywords (phrase or exact). Never cast a broad net and pay to learn.
- Every day: if the budget isn't being spent, raise bids 10-20% until the ads show at the top.
  Add lower-intent keywords only when the top keywords can't spend the budget.
- Every day (10-20 min): add about 5 high-intent keywords and 15-20 negatives. Pause anything that misses the KPI
  (cost per lead, then cost per sale).

## 4. Position 1 or nothing (impression share)
- Ad position 1 gets about 2x the traffic of position 4 and takes 70-80% of ad clicks.
- For the coliseum keywords, aim for **80-85% search impression share**. Raise bids and budget to get there.
- Split the budget: **80% to a campaign with only the coliseum keywords**, 20% to the wider net.
- First Monday of every month (1 hour): match sold policies to the keyword or search term that produced them
  (GHL + quote-form attribution + utm_term) and move budget to the keywords that make the most money, not
  the ones with the cheapest leads.
- With a small budget ($30-40/day): apply this to 1-2 coliseum keywords in a tight set of states
  before spreading out.

## 5. Don't take the Google rep's call
- Keep **auto-apply recommendations OFF**. No Performance Max, AI Max, broad match, Display or Search Partners
  "because the rep said so".
- Test a new feature only after: the coliseum keywords spend the full budget, impression share
  is about 85%, and every coliseum keyword has its own landing page.
- Fundamentals check: best ads? Ad matches the landing page? Tracking shows which keyword makes money?
  80% of budget on the winners?

## 6. Competitor terms ("break their legs") — with care
- Bid on competitors' brand terms (other brokers or online marketplaces). Use 5-10% of the search budget,
  aimed at the 3 competitors with the most searches (check volume in Keyword Planner).
- **Never put a trademarked name in the ad text.** Use "Get a second opinion before you enroll"
  or "Compare before you sign".
- Give each competitor its own landing page: an honest comparison table, our proof (reviews, licensed agents,
  "1,000+ companies assisted", 7-day support), and no trash talk.
- Insurance caution: don't bid on carrier names in a way that implies we *are* the carrier.
  Check Google's trademark policy and state rules first.

## Checklist for any new Google Ads build
1. Pick the coliseum keywords from data (or the best guess, marked as such) and list them.
2. Halo research notes (pains, fears, words drivers and owners actually use).
3. Ads that sell the click and stay compliant. Pin the keyword headline.
4. Phrase and exact match only, with negatives from day one. Auto-apply off.
5. Budget split 80 coliseum / 20 wider (plus 5-10% competitor once the core works).
6. Each coliseum keyword gets its own landing page, with tracking (utm_term={keyword}, conversion at the phone step).
7. Daily bonsai routine + monthly revenue-by-keyword review.
