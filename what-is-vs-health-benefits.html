About VS Health Benefits FAQ

What is VS Health Benefits?

VS Health Benefits is a licensed independent health insurance brokerage based in Miami, FL. We help individuals, families, self-employed workers, and small businesses in 40+ states compare plans and find affordable coverage at no cost.

Does VS Health Benefits charge a fee?

No. Our service is completely free to you because we are paid by the insurance carriers, not by our clients. You get expert help comparing and enrolling at no extra cost.

What does independent brokerage mean?

It means we are not tied to a single insurance company. We compare plans across all the top carriers to find the best fit for you, rather than pushing one company.

Where does VS Health Benefits operate?

We are based in Miami, FL and licensed in more than 40 states, so we can help clients across most of the country by phone, email, and video.

Who founded VS Health Benefits?

VS Health Benefits was founded by Bradley Vilsaint, who built the company and its technology from the ground up with a focus on honest, person-first service.
