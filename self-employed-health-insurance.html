How self-employed health insurance quotes work

  1. Enter your home ZIP

    Plans and prices depend on the Florida county you live in, so your ZIP decides which plans you can get.

  2. Answer a few quick questions

    Who needs coverage and their ages, tobacco use, and your estimated household income. For self-employed people that means net profit, not gross sales.

  3. A licensed advisor checks your quote

    We compare plans side by side, check your doctors and prescriptions, and make sure your income estimate gets you the right tax credit. We reach out within 1 business day, often within hours.

What sets the price of your quote

  • Home ZIP and age. Under ACA rules these two drive most of the price. Your health history does not.
  • Net income. Tax credits shrink as income rises. A good year can change your price, so we plan for it with you.
  • Network. HMOs usually cost less; PPOs let you see more doctors without referrals.
  • Plan level and tobacco. Bronze, silver and gold trade a lower premium for a higher deductible, and tobacco use can add a surcharge.

Self-employed health insurance FAQ

How fast can I get a self-employed health insurance quote?

The online form takes about a minute. A licensed advisor follows up within 1 business day, usually within hours, by call or text.

Do quotes cost anything or lock me into a plan?

No. Quotes are free and there is no obligation. Insurance companies pay us, and you pay the same premium you would pay buying the plan yourself.

What income do I use if I'm self-employed?

Use your expected net self-employment income for the year (what is left after business expenses), not your gross sales. If it changes during the year, you update it so your tax credit stays accurate.

Can I deduct my health insurance premiums?

Many self-employed people can deduct premiums with the self-employed health insurance deduction, as long as they are not eligible for an employer plan through a job or a spouse. Check with your tax preparer for your situation.

When can I enroll in a 2027 plan?

Open enrollment for 2027 coverage runs November 1, 2026 to January 15, 2027 in Florida. Enroll by December 15 for coverage that starts January 1. Outside that window you need a qualifying life event, such as losing other coverage.

Can I cover my spouse and kids too?

Yes. You can put your whole household on one individual and family plan, and the tax credit is based on your total household income.
