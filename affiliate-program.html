Program rules, in plain English

  • Flat reward, never tied to a sale. Each qualified referral earns the same fixed amount, whether or not the person buys coverage and whatever plan they choose (s. 626.112(8), Florida Statutes). Your reward amount is confirmed when you're approved.
  • What counts as qualified. The person came through your link, is 18 or older, is new to VS Health Benefits, agreed to be contacted, and completes a free consultation with one of our licensed advisors.
  • Talk about your experience, not plans or prices. Share your story and your link. Please don't quote premiums, describe plan benefits, promise "free" coverage or help anyone fill out an application. That is regulated work our licensed advisors do.
  • Always disclose. Label sponsored or affiliate posts clearly (for example #ad or #partner) as the FTC requires.
  • Fine print. One reward per referred person. No paid ads bidding on our brand name. Rewards may be reportable income. Where state law does not allow referral rewards, we can still help your referral but cannot pay a reward. We may change or end the program at any time; referrals that already qualified will be paid.

Creator program questions

How much do I earn per referral?

Every qualified referral earns the same flat reward. We confirm your amount when your application is approved. It never depends on whether the person buys a plan or which plan they choose.

Do I need a license or insurance experience?

No. You share your link and your own experience. Our licensed advisors handle all quoting, plan advice and enrollment.

How is my link tracked?

Your link carries your personal code. When someone visits through it and later asks for a quote on the site, they're credited to you, even if they come back a few days later on the same device.

Is there a follower minimum?

No. We look for creators whose audience needs health coverage: truck drivers, 1099 and gig workers, small business owners, families and Spanish-speaking communities. Engagement matters more than follower count.

What can I say in my content?

Talk about your own experience and why you'd recommend talking to a licensed advisor. Don't quote prices, describe plan benefits, say coverage is free, or help someone fill out an application. Label sponsored posts with #ad or #partner.

When do I get paid?

Once a referral qualifies (they complete a free consultation with a licensed advisor). We pay out qualified referrals on a regular schedule and let you know what you've earned.
