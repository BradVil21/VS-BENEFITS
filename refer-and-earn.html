Who can earn

One simple requirement to collect referral pay

To receive referral payouts, you must be a VS Health Benefits client. Becoming a client is easy and costs you nothing extra.

  • Be a VS Health Benefits client. Have your own coverage set up through our brokerage. That is what makes you eligible to earn.
  • Create a free referral account. Sign up, confirm your profile, and you are ready to send referrals.

Questions

Refer & Earn FAQ

How much can I earn per referral?

Referral pay depends on the coverage your referral enrolls in. We share the details when you sign up, and your portal shows the amount earned on each referral as it moves to paid.

When do I get paid?

Referral pay is released once your referral's application is approved and they become a VS Health Benefits client. You will see the status update live in your member portal.

Do I need a license to refer people?

No. You are simply introducing someone who needs coverage. Our licensed advisors handle all of the insurance work, paperwork, and enrollment.

Why do I have to be a client to earn?

Referral pay is reserved for members of the VS Health Benefits family. Becoming a client just means we set up your own coverage through our brokerage, which is free guidance and often saves you money.

Is there a limit on how many people I can refer?

None at all. Refer as many people as you like. The more enrolled referrals you send, the more you earn.

How much money can I make referring health insurance?

There is no cap on what you can earn. You get a referral payout for every person you refer who enrolls in coverage, so the more people you refer, the more money you make. Many members treat it as easy extra income on top of their day-to-day life.

Can I earn money from home?

Yes. You can refer people and track your earnings entirely from your phone or computer. There is nothing to sell and no appointments to run. You make the introduction, we do the work, and you get paid.

Is it really free to join the referral program?

Completely free. There is no cost to create your account, set up your profile, or send referrals. You only need to be a VS Health Benefits client, which is also free guidance that usually saves you money on your own coverage.

How do I get started?

Create your free account, set up your profile, and submit your first referral. If you are not a client yet, request a quote and we will help you get covered first.
