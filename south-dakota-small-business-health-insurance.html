How does small business health insurance work?

A business with at least one W-2 employee besides the owner can offer a group plan. Carriers typically require around 70% of eligible employees to participate and the employer to contribute roughly half of the employee-only premium. Group coverage is guaranteed-issue regardless of anyone's health, premiums are a deductible business expense, and small employers may qualify for a tax credit. Businesses with fewer than 50 employees are not required to offer coverage.

Rules to Know

Small Business Health Insurance Rules and Participation

A few rules shape how small group health insurance works in every state. Here is what employers should know before shopping for a plan.

Minimum Participation

Most carriers require 50% to 75% of eligible employees to enroll. Employees covered through a spouse can usually waive without counting against you.

Guaranteed Issue

Small group plans for 1 to 50 employees are guaranteed issue under federal law. Carriers cannot deny coverage or charge more because of anyone’s health.

Carriers Vary by State

Which carriers and networks you can pick from depends on your state and county. We compare Blue Cross Blue Shield, UnitedHealthcare, Aetna, Cigna and regional carriers for your ZIP code.

Small Business Tax Credit

Businesses with fewer than 25 full-time equivalent employees and average wages under $56,000 may qualify for a federal Small Business Health Care Tax Credit worth up to 50% of your premium contribution. We check this for every client.

Small Business Health Insurance FAQ

Can a small business in South Dakota get group health insurance with only 2 employees?+

In most states you need as few as 2 employees to qualify for a small group health plan. Most carriers require at least one W-2 employee in addition to the owner. VS Health Benefits specializes in micro-group plans for businesses with 2 to 9 employees -- a segment most brokers overlook.

How many employees do I need for group health insurance?+

In most states you need as few as 2 employees to qualify for a small group health plan. Most carriers require at least one W-2 employee in addition to the owner. VS Health Benefits specializes in micro-group plans for businesses with 2 to 9 employees -- a segment most brokers overlook.

How much does small business health insurance cost?+

Employers typically contribute $300 to $600 per employee per month for a solid group plan, depending on the state, ages and plan design. Most employers cover 50% to 75% of the employee-only premium. Dependent coverage is typically paid by the employee. We run exact quotes for your team at no cost.

When can I enroll in a small business health plan?+

Unlike individual plans, small group health insurance has no fixed open enrollment window. You can set up a new group plan at any time of year. Most plans take effect on the first of the month following enrollment -- this is one of the biggest advantages of group coverage over individual plans.

Is small business health insurance tax deductible?+

Yes. Employer premium contributions are generally 100% tax deductible as a business expense. Employees benefit too because their share of premiums is often paid pre-tax through a Section 125 cafeteria plan. Businesses with fewer than 25 FTE employees may also qualify for the Small Business Health Care Tax Credit worth up to 50% of the employer premium contribution.

What is the best health insurance for a small business?+

The best plan depends on your team size, industry, employee ages, and how much you want to contribute. Your state’s Blue Cross Blue Shield plan often has the broadest local network. UnitedHealthcare and Aetna are strong options if employees travel or work across multiple states. For groups of 2 to 9, micro-group plans through select carriers offer competitive group rates. We compare all options side by side for free.

Do I have to offer health insurance to my employees?+

Employers with fewer than 50 full-time equivalent employees are not required by federal law to offer health insurance. However, offering group coverage is one of the most effective tools to attract and retain employees -- especially in trades, hospitality, and healthcare where competition for workers is fierce. Many owners find that turnover costs exceed the cost of the plan.

Do you work with small businesses in my state?+

Yes. VS Health Benefits is licensed in 40+ states and works with small businesses from coast to coast. Plan availability and carrier networks vary by state and county, so we quote your exact ZIP code and show you which carriers have the strongest networks where your team lives and works.
